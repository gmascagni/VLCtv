How to Use Playlists with VLC Media Player

⚡

Enable 1-Click VLC Launch from Browser

To open channels in VLC with a single click from Chrome, Edge, or Firefox:

Double-click Enable_VLC_OneClick.bat in this folder once. It registers the vlc:// protocol for your user account (no admin rights needed). When your browser asks, check "Always allow" and VLC will launch immediately!

Even without registration, clicking "Open in VLC" automatically downloads that channel's .m3u8 file (click it in your browser downloads to play) and copies the stream URL to your clipboard.

1

Download or Locate Your Playlist

Download US_All_Channels.m3u8 for the complete catalog, or download category-specific playlists like US_News_Channels.m3u8, US_Movies_Channels.m3u8, or US_Sports_Channels.m3u8.

2

Open the Playlist in VLC

Open VLC Media Player. Drag and drop the .m3u8 or .xspf file directly into the VLC window, or press Ctrl + O and select the playlist.

You can also double-click any of the provided Open_In_VLC.bat launcher scripts.

3

Enable Collapsible Category Grouping

In VLC, press Ctrl + L to open the Playlist View. Right-click the column headers or open View > Playlist View Mode and select Grouped by Category / Tree View. VLC will display News, Movies, Sports, and Entertainment as neat folders!

4

Optimize Network Caching for Smooth Playback

To avoid buffering stutter on live HLS streams: Go to Tools > Preferences (Ctrl + P) > Click Show Settings: All (bottom left) > Go to Input / Codecs > Scroll to Advanced > Set Network caching (ms) to 1500 or 2000 ms.

5

View Channel Descriptions & TV Program Guide (EPG)

Channel Synopsis: Press Ctrl + I (Current Media Information) on any playing channel to view its complete description and genre details embedded in the playlist.

Program Guide (EPG): Press Shift + G or select Tools > Program Guide to open VLC's built-in TV schedule window displaying currently airing shows and upcoming programs!

Subtitles & English Translation Guide

AI

Chrome & Edge Real-Time Live Speech Translation

Your web browser (Google Chrome & Microsoft Edge) can transcribe foreign speech and translate it into English subtitles directly on your screen in real time:

In Chrome: Open Chrome Settings (chrome://settings/accessibility) → Toggle on Live Caption → Enable Live Translate → Choose English as target language.

In Edge: Settings → Accessibility → Turn on Live Captions.

Once enabled, any Spanish, French, or foreign broadcast displays automatic live English subtitles overlaid on your screen!

CC

Embedded Broadcast Captions & SAP English Audio

Many channels broadcast official closed captions or Secondary Audio Programming (SAP) dubbing:

In Web Preview: Click the [CC] Subtitles button directly under the video player. If the stream carries embedded CEA-608 / WebVTT caption tracks, they toggle on automatically.

In VLC Media Player: Press V to toggle/cycle subtitles, and press B to cycle audio tracks to English SAP!

🇺🇸

Prefer Watching Only English Channels?

You can instantly hide all foreign-language channels (Spanish, French, Arabic, etc.) with one click using the English Only button at the top of the channel guide.
